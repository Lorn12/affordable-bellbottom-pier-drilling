● Project Preparation

What to Know Before Pier Drilling Begins

Project requirements vary by location and site conditions. These details help clarify jobsite responsibilities before work is scheduled.

The client or general contractor is responsible for permits, pier layout, and rebar cages.

Spoil removal and jobsite cleanup are handled by the client or general contractor.

Equipment access and drilling in standard soil or rock depend on the location and site conditions.

Mobilization and minimum fees may apply depending on the project and location.
