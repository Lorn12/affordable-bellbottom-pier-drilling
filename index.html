● Equipment & Capabilities

Compact Equipment for Tight-Access Pier Drilling

Affordable Bellbottom uses skid steers and a compact excavator for straight-shaft and belled pier drilling on residential and commercial jobsites. Equipment and drilling tools are selected based on site access, project requirements, pier dimensions, and ground conditions.

  • Tight-Access Jobsites

    Compact equipment can access gated yards and limited side-yard spaces, subject to the conditions of each jobsite.

  • Soil & Rock Drilling

    Equipment and drilling tools are selected based on the project requirements, location, and whether the site contains standard soil or rock.

  • Concrete Pour Support

    A modified bucket can be used to assist the concrete contractor with pouring the drilled piers.

● How We Work

Clear Coordination From Project Review Through Drilling

We review the available project information, confirm site and access requirements, and coordinate the drilling scope with the customer, contractor, or project team before work begins.

  1. 01 — Review the Project

    We review plans, pier requirements, project location, access conditions, and known soil or rock conditions to understand the drilling scope.

  2. 02 — Confirm Responsibilities

    We clarify what Affordable Bellbottom will handle and what remains with the client or general contractor, including permits, layout, rebar cages, spoil removal, and cleanup.

  3. 03 — Coordinate the Work

    We coordinate drilling needs, site access, and project timing with the customer or contractor so everyone understands the next steps before mobilization.

  4. 04 — Support the Next Phase

    After drilling is complete, we can assist the concrete contractor with pouring the piers using a modified bucket when that support is part of the project scope.

● Project Planning

The Right Details Help Us Prepare for the Work

Every drilling project is different. Sharing accurate site information, plans, access conditions, and pier requirements helps us review the scope before scheduling and mobilization.

Discuss Your Project
Project Location
Provide the jobsite address so we can review service-area availability, access conditions, and mobilization requirements.
Plans & Pier Requirements
Share available plans, pier count, diameter, depth, bell dimensions, and any engineering requirements you have.
Site Access & Ground Conditions
Let us know about gates, side-yard access, limited work areas, or known soil and rock conditions that may affect equipment setup or drilling.
Project Responsibilities
Permits, layout, rebar cages, spoil removal, and cleanup are handled by the client or general contractor unless otherwise confirmed before the project.
