● Service Overview

Straight-Shaft Drilling for Foundation Projects

Straight-shaft pier drilling prepares vertical holes for foundation piers before rebar and concrete are placed. We provide this service for residential and commercial projects across Houston and the Greater Houston area.

  • Residential and commercial foundation work
  • Direct client and subcontracted projects
  • Drilling based on provided plans and site conditions

● Jobsite Capabilities

Compact Equipment for Tight-Access Jobsites

Our skid steer equipment can access gated yards and tight side yards, subject to site conditions. Straight-shaft drilling is available in standard soil or rock depending on the project location and ground conditions.

  • Gated-yard and tight-side-yard access
  • Standard soil or rock drilling
  • Access evaluated according to site conditions
